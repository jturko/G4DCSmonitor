3.1 GEOMETRY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Outer profile: a WIDE, SHALLOW RECTANGLE (a flat slab seen end-on).
  Its width is dimensioned "10mm"; its height is small - as drawn,
  height : width is approximately 1 : 3.5 to 1 : 4, i.e. of order 2.5-3 mm

* Fill: the rectangle interior is filled with a MID/DARK GREY tone which

* FOUR CIRCLES are drawn inside this rectangle, in a SINGLE HORIZONTAL ROW,
  equally spaced, all of IDENTICAL DIAMETER, all with their centres on the
  same horizontal line = the mid-height of the slab.
  - The circles are filled LIGHT GREY (lighter than the surrounding grease

5. CONSOLIDATED GEOMETRY SUMMARY FOR CAD / GEANT4 CONSTRUCTION
=============================================================================
Coordinate convention suggested:
   +Z along the fibre axis, Z = 0 at the SiPM-coupled end face,
   Z = 250 mm at the far end.
   +X across the fibre array (the dimensioned 10 mm direction).
   +Y through the thin direction of the ribbon (thickness, undimensioned).

SOLIDS TO BUILD
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) WLS FIBRES x 4
    - G4Tubs (solid cylinder) or concentric core+cladding tubes.
    - Axis along Z, length 250 mm (full holder length), both ends flat and
      perpendicular to Z.
    - Circular cross-section, diameter D (from body text; drawn as
      approximately 0.20-0.22 x 10 mm, i.e. ~2 mm).
    - Placed at Y = 0 (common mid-plane); X positions equally spaced and
      symmetric about X = 0, pitch P ~ 2.3-2.5 mm as scaled from the
      cross-section (take exact value from body text).
      Nominal X centres for 4 fibres at pitch P:
          X = -1.5P, -0.5P, +0.5P, +1.5P
      With P = 2.5 mm: X = -3.75, -1.25, +1.25, +3.75 mm, giving an outer
      envelope of 7.5 mm + D = ~9.5 mm, i.e. just inside the 10 mm width,
      leaving a thin grease film (~0.25 mm) at each side. This is exactly
      what the cross-section drawing shows.
    - Both end faces are FLAT and PERPENDICULAR to Z (square-cut, polished).
      The Z = 0 face is co-planar with the holder end face and with the
      optical-grease/SiPM coupling plane.
    - If building a realistic WLS fibre: concentric structure
          core (polystyrene, n ~ 1.59, doped with wavelength shifter)
          inner cladding (PMMA, n ~ 1.49, ~3 % of radius)
          outer cladding (fluorinated polymer, n ~ 1.42, ~3 % of radius)
      The drawing shows a single flat-shaded circle only; multi-cladding is
      an assumption to be confirmed from the body text.
    - NO BENDS, NO TAPER, NO HELIX: perfectly straight and parallel over the
      full 250 mm, confirmed by both the sketch and the photograph.

(2) OPTICAL GREASE VOLUME
    - A G4Box (rectangular slab) of dimensions:
          Z (length)    = 250 mm  (co-extensive with the fibres/holder)
          X (width)     = 10 mm   (the dimensioned value, interior width)
          Y (thickness) = T_grease, NOT dimensioned; as scaled from the
                          cross-section the slab height is ~0.27-0.29 of its
                          10 mm width, i.e. ~2.7-2.9 mm. Take the exact value
                          from the body text.
    - The four fibre cylinders are SUBTRACTED from (or placed as daughters
      inside) this box. In Geant4 the natural construction is:
          grease box = mother logical volume
          4 fibre cylinders placed as daughter volumes inside it
      which automatically carves the grease around them.
    - KEY POINT ON FIBRE IMMERSION / "PROTRUSION INTO THE GREASE":
        * The fibres are FULLY IMMERSED. They do NOT protrude through any
          grease surface anywhere along the 250 mm length.
        * In the X direction: the two outermost fibres stop short of the
          slab side walls, leaving a thin grease film of order 0.1-0.3 mm.
        * In the Y direction: each fibre's upper and lower tangent lines sit
          a small distance below/above the slab's top and bottom faces. As
          scaled, the fibre diameter spans ~70-85 % of the slab thickness,
          so the residual grease layer above and below each fibre is
          approximately (T_grease - D)/2, i.e. of order 0.2-0.4 mm each side.
        * Between adjacent fibres the circles come close but do not touch;
          a thin grease bridge (~0.3-0.5 mm) separates neighbouring fibres.
        * Therefore every fibre is surrounded on ALL sides (±X, ±Y) by a
          continuous, closed film of grease. Optical contact is total; there
          are no air gaps anywhere in the cross-section.
        * At Z = 0 and Z = 250 mm the fibre end faces are FLUSH with the
          grease block end faces (co-planar) - the fibres neither protrude
          out of nor recede into the grease at the ends.

(3) WLS FIBRE HOLDER / CLAMP
    - In the long broadside view the holder appears as the two DARK GREY
      bands at the extreme top and bottom of the 10 mm stack, each ~8-10 %
      of the 10 mm (i.e. ~0.8-1.0 mm thick as drawn), running the full
      250 mm. These are the two jaws that sandwich the fibre+grease ribbon.
    - In the end-on cross-section the holder is the dark boundary of the grey
      slab. The photograph shows the real part: a WHITE, OPEN / SEMI-OPEN
      U-CHANNEL that cradles the fibres from below and from both sides,
      leaving the top surface of the fibres visible (hence the green glow is
      seen directly in the photo).
    - Suggested CAD construction: a G4Box of outer dimensions
          Z = 250 mm
          X = 10 mm + 2 x W_wall
          Y = T_grease + 2 x T_wall
      with a central rectangular pocket (10 mm x T_grease x 250 mm)
      subtracted, into which the grease+fibre assembly is inserted.
      Wall thickness W_wall / T_wall not dimensioned; scale ~0.8-1.0 mm from
      the long view, or take from body text.
    - Material: white 3D-printed polymer, visible FDM layer lines in the
      photograph (horizontal striations). Likely PLA/ABS/PETG, WHITE, which
      in Geant4 should be modelled as a DIFFUSE (Lambertian) REFLECTOR
      (G4OpticalSurface, unified model, groundfrontpainted or
      polishedfrontpainted with high reflectivity ~0.9-0.95) rather than an
      absorber, since the white plastic will recycle escaping photons back
      into the fibres.
    - The holder ends are cut SQUARE at both Z = 0 and Z = 250 mm; the
      photograph confirms a flat, perpendicular left-hand mating face.

(4) SiPM
    - A thin rectangular block (G4Box) butt-coupled FLUSH to the Z = 0 end
      face of the fibre/grease/holder assembly, centred on X = 0, Y = 0.
    - Its active face spans the full 10 mm array width (sketch shows the
      green block height approximately equal to, or very slightly greater
      than, the 10 mm dimension), so a SINGLE SiPM views ALL FOUR fibre end
      faces simultaneously.
    - Its thickness along Z is small: as drawn roughly 1/6 to 1/8 of its
      10 mm height, i.e. ~1.2-1.7 mm (package thickness; the sensitive
      epoxy/silicon layer is thinner still).
    - A thin optical-grease coupling layer should be placed between the fibre
      end faces and the SiPM window (not explicitly drawn, but implied by the
      "Optical grease" label and standard practice). Thickness ~0.05-0.2 mm.
    - There is NO second SiPM at Z = 250 mm. The far end is unread; it is
      either left open (absorbing), blackened, or mirrored - the figures do
      not specify. Default assumption for simulation: specify from text; if
      unknown, model as an absorbing/open end.

(5) SCINTILLATOR TILE (from FIG. 3(b), for the full Config-4 assembly)
    - A square tile (plan view) whose edge length is 4 x the grid-cell pitch.
      Edge length and thickness are NOT dimensioned in these figures - take
      from the body text.
    - The fibre clamp ribbon (250 mm long) lies along ONE FULL EDGE of the
      square tile, flat against it, with its 250 mm axis parallel to that
      edge. Since the bar spans the entire tile edge in the sketch, the tile
      edge length is ~250 mm, i.e. a 250 x 250 mm square tile.
    - The ribbon's THIN direction (Y, ~2.7-2.9 mm) is normal to the tile

    - The single SiPM sits at ONE END (the sketch shows the left/top-left

    - The three ORANGE "regions of interest" are notional irradiation /
